# MangoSnake

A modern version of the classic arcade game.

Snake v3.1 updates the original game with a bright neon look and online play. Built for casual fun and competitive high scores on your phone.

* Download from **Google Play** and get a skin for FREE: https://id.orangomango.org/games/mangosnake

## 🌟 WHAT’S NEW IN v3.1?
Enjoy a glowing snake, pulsing mangoes, and a clean UI. Now featuring 50+ snake and mango skins! Unlock them by finishing achievements or spending MangoCoins earned while you play.

## 🏆 GLOBAL RANKINGS & STATS
Beat the world! Log in with your MangoGames ID to save your progress and climb the live leaderboards at 🔗 id.orangomango.org/games/mangosnake.
* **Google Play Games**: Unlock new achievements as you play.
* **Overall Stats**: Track your progress and view your lifetime game data.

## 🎮 GAME MODES
* **4 Speeds**: Choose from Easy, Medium, Hard, or Extreme.
* **Custom Games**: Pick your own board size and snake speed.
* **Screen Wrapping**: Toggle the ability to pass through screen edges.
* 💻 **Auto-Play**: Watch the snake solve the game perfectly using Hamiltonian Cycles.

## ✨ KEY FEATURES
* **Neon Style**: Vibrant colors and smooth animations.
* **Easy Controls**: Smooth touch controls for precise movement.
* **Fast & Light**: Runs perfectly on all Android devices.
* **Customization**: Many new snake and mango skins.
